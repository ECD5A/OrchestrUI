# Code of Conduct

OrchestrUI welcomes respectful technical collaboration.

Expected: discuss code and ideas rather than attacking people; give specific feedback; respect project scope and maintainers' time; credit relevant work; protect private information.

Unacceptable: harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, doxxing, malicious payloads disguised as reports, or repeated disruption after a maintainer asks it to stop.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the project and contributors.

To report a concern privately, email [stelmak159@gmail.com](mailto:stelmak159@gmail.com). Include only the context needed to understand what happened; you do not need to confront another participant first.
