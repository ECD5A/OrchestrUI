# Support

Questions are welcome, including setup questions and unexpected routing results.

- [Discussions](https://github.com/ECD5A/OrchestrUI/discussions) — usage questions, ideas and help choosing an approach.
- [Issues](https://github.com/ECD5A/OrchestrUI/issues/new/choose) — bugs and feature requests. Share what you tried and what you expected; a small example helps.
- [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) — the path to your first PR.

You do not need a proposed fix to report a problem. Remove credentials and private project data from examples. Suspected vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

For direct contact, see the [README](README.md#contact).
